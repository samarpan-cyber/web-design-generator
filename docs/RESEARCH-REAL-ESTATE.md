# Real estate design research and how it shaped the templates

Research was done with web searches (agency blogs and vendor guides, so treat the claims as informed opinion, not measured results). What was found, and what we did with it.

## What the sources agree on
- **Booking a visit must be close.** The most common failure pattern is a beautiful homepage that hides the visit or enquiry three clicks deep. Every design keeps "Book a Site Visit" in the header, the hero, the footer band and a dedicated page, and two designs put the enquiry form in the hero.
- **Full bleed imagery, used with restraint.** Large photography, subtle parallax and hover effects are praised; heavy effects everywhere are not, and mobile speed matters. Effects here use transforms only, run only for elements on screen, switch off on small screens and for reduced motion, and photos are about 150 KB each.
- **Search and filters are the core of a listings site.** Location, size and status filters, with lead capture next to them. The listings designs filter live in the browser, and filters appear only when the data has something to filter.
- **A payment calculator brings repeat visits** but is not a lead tool on its own, and needs a disclaimer. Our estimator takes the visitor's own numbers and says "indicative only".
- **Commercial buyers and tenants want complete facts, floor plans, maps and short forms.** The commercial designs add space type tabs, a space calculator (headcount times sq ft per person, both editable) and a short enquiry form.
- **India specific: RERA.** A project page should show the project's RERA number, and buyers should verify it on the state portal. When a number is found on the site it is shown with a reminder to verify it; it is never invented.
- **WordPress themes (Houzez, WpResidence and similar) follow a familiar pattern:** slider hero with a tabbed search form, featured listings grid, agents, testimonials. The two plain designs follow it.

## Kinds of real estate business, and what changes
| Kind | Reads like | Designs favoured |
|---|---|---|
| Residential developer | Projects, status, sizes, possession, RERA | Homestead, Atelier, Developer Classic |
| Luxury | Few residences, editorial, privacy | Atelier, Skyline |
| Commercial | Spaces, leasing, area, tours | Workplace, Skyline, Developer Classic |
| Plots and land | Layouts, site visits, title and approvals | Terra, Developer Classic |
| Residential and commercial | Both | Listings, Workplace, Homestead |
| Broker or agency | Listings, filters, search first | Listings, Property Search |

## Honesty rules (same recipe as clinics)
- Prices, areas, sizes, status, amenities, RERA numbers, locations and testimonials appear only if the website or the details supplied contain them.
- Without projects, designs show property types (apartments, offices, plots and so on) instead of fake listings.
- Stock photos fill gaps and are captioned "Illustrative image"; the prospect's own photos always come first.
- The buying and leasing checklists are general guidance and say so.
